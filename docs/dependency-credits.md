# Local dependency credits

CampusHub stores these browser libraries locally so QR passes and camera scanning work without Composer, a CDN, or an internet connection:

- **QRCode.js** by davidshimjs — QR image generation, MIT License. Source: <https://github.com/davidshimjs/qrcodejs>. License copy: `assets/vendor/qrcodejs-LICENSE.txt`.
- **jsQR** by Cosmo Wolfe and contributors — camera-frame QR decoding, Apache License 2.0. Source: <https://github.com/cozmo/jsQR>. License copy: `assets/vendor/jsQR-LICENSE.txt`.

Certificate PDFs are produced by the project’s small purpose-built writer in `includes/certificate.php`; it has no third-party runtime dependency.
